GENEALOGY OF MORALS

First Essay: The Pathogenesis of Moral Values

Analyzing the linguistic and psychological inversion that created "Good and Evil."

The Knightly-Aristocratic Method

  • Origin: Self-affirmation.
  • Definition: Noble, powerful, beautiful.
  • Opposition: "Bad" as a secondary afterthought.

The Sacerdotal-Priestly Method

  • Origin: Ressentiment/Reaction.
  • Definition: "Evil" as the primary target (the strong).
  • Opposition: "Good" as the residue (the weak).
  • Etymology: Latin malus vs Greek melas (dark/common).
  • Free Will: A psychological invention to hold the "strong" accountable for their nature.
